    When a water test fails

    Under DOH AO 2013-0003, a unit whose result exceeds the limit stops operating and notifies patients until a repeat test complies. Our procedure gets you back to a passing result, on record.

    1. 1Lab alert

      Result over the limit; the unit pauses per the DOH rule.

    2. 2TYCUS on site

      Priority call-out through our 24/7 support line.

    3. 3Fix the cause

      Clean or replace membranes; disinfect the loop and machine hoses.

    4. 4Retest

      Re-sampling coordinated with your accredited laboratory.

    5. 5Reopen

      Passing result; corrective action report filed for DOH.

    Who takes the samples. TYCUS does not collect DOH water samples. In NCR and nearby provinces only DOH-accredited laboratory staff may sample, so we schedule PM and disinfection around your laboratory's monthly visit.

    FAQ

    Questions clinics ask us

    Our technician already handles the RO.

    Good. We support them with parts, trend analysis and a monthly report from a licensed service company, which is what DOH asks to see.

    We already have a supplier.

    Keep them for comparison. Ask both of us for last quarter's trend logs and see which would have warned you before a culture failed.

    Is monthly PM worth the cost?

    Compare it with the sessions lost if the unit stops for a retest. The calculator near the top of this page does the sum for your unit.

    Our water tests are passing.

    Under 200 CFU/mL is the license line. We aim to keep you under 50, so one bad month does not turn into a shutdown.

    Can we just call you when something breaks?

    You can, but by then patients are being rescheduled. A monthly visit finds a fouling membrane or an exhausted carbon bed weeks earlier.

    Who collects the DOH water samples?

    An accredited laboratory. In NCR and nearby provinces only DOH-accredited laboratory staff may sample, so we plan our visits around theirs.
