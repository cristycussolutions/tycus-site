How we work

We check the drawing before anything gets built.

Two specialist factories build our heaters and sensors. We lock the spec with you first, so what arrives is exactly what you ordered.

  1. Application reviewWe look at what is being heated or measured, the temperature, the power available, and how it is installed.
  2. SpecificationWe set the specification with you and check the technical drawing before anything is built.
  3. Built to orderOur manufacturing partners build each item to the agreed specification.
  4. Delivery and supportWe handle delivery and stay on as your local point of support.
